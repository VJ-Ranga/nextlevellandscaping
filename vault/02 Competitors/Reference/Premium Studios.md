## Patterns
- Word count ↔ prestige: near-perfect inverse correlation. Tier A 90–99% imagery.
- Work first, not value prop. Headline = identity statement ("X is a … studio"), not benefit claim.
- Process always low (band 5–7), never above fold.
- **0/15 show pricing or ask budget.** (We override for trade reasons — see [[Design Direction]].)
- Credibility "findable, not announced": awards in nav, one membership mark, establishment year.
- Star ratings, stat bars, suburb lists, financing = mass-market tells.

## Transferable ideas we took
- Project cards labelled suburb + year + services
- One philosophy paragraph, not three
- Process low on page, 4–5 phases
- Credentials as a quiet line, not vanity count-ups (we keep a trust line — AU evidence)
