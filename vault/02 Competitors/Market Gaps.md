## Strongest single idea
**Render → build.** Whole pitch is "see it in 2D & 3D before you commit" — nobody shows the artefact. Side-by-side render + finished photo would stand alone. Needs render files → [[Waiting on Client]].

## Nuance on Current Work
Premium studios that show live work ([[Premium Studios|Peachy Green]] "In Progress", [[Nathan Burkett]] filter) present it as **portfolio in a different tense** (renders + names), not as a status dashboard. Our stage tracker = more transparent, less premium. Client decision → [[Open Questions]].
