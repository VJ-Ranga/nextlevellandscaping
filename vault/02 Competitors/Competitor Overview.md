# Competitor Overview

Research 2026-09-09: 3 parallel agents — (1) 19 AU landscaping sites (12 Adelaide), (2) 15 premium/global studios, (3) 2025–26 web standards. Source: `RESEARCH-REPORT.md` in repo.

## Two playbooks
| | Premium studios (n=15) | AU trade market (n=19) |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage length | **2–9 sections** | **~11** (modal) |
| FAQ on home | 0 of top studios | 16% — but both best SA sites have one |
| Licence number | never | 11% — top SA trust site shows it |
| Reviews | 0–4, text only | 32%, stars normal |
| Suburb lists | omit | 37% |
| Budget field on form | 0/15 | present on SA trade sites |
| Opening move | the **work** | benefit claim + phone |

## Modal SA order
`Hero → who we are → services (4–5) → portfolio (suburb-tagged) → why us → [process] → [testimonials] → [FAQ] → trust logos → CTA → footer`

- Trade nav: **Home · About · Services · Projects · Contact** + Get a Quote. Studios: Projects → Practice, often no "Home".
- CTA wording: "Get a free quote" 32% · "Enquire" 26% · "Contact us" 21% · phone 21% · "Book a consult" 11%.
