1. Hero H1 duplicated word: "Bring Your Garden to Life **Life**".
2. "over a decade" vs "decades" — inconsistent, no founding year.
3. Team section looks placeholder → [[Team]].
4. All 8 `/service/*` pages are empty stubs (thin-content SEO liability).
5. No trading hours, no map, no Google Business Profile link; email hidden in privacy policy.
6. No meta descriptions anywhere; `og:locale` = `en_US` (should be `en_AU`).
7. Only one social channel (Instagram).
8. BLD licence + MLSA shown weakly.
9. Store product pages thin (P.O.A only).

11. Client's main complaint: layout is **"boxy"** (div-in-div coloured blocks) → [[Design Direction]].
