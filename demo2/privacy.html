Privacy & Security

This page explains how NextLevel Landscaping handles your information when you visit this website.

What we collect

If you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address and phone number so we can respond to your enquiry. This information is shared only with NextLevel Landscaping staff and is never sold to third parties.

Cookies

This website stores one localStorage value — your selected visual theme preference. No tracking cookies or advertising cookies are used.

Third-party services

Our site loads fonts from Google Fonts, icons from Font Awesome via CDN, and videos from locally hosted files. These services have their own privacy policies.

Security

This is a demo website. A production version of this site should be served over HTTPS and hosted with a provider that applies up-to-date security patches.

Contact

For privacy concerns, contact us at info@nextlevellandscaping.com.au.
