
Grange Luxury Landscaping Project – By NextLevel Landscaping

NextLevel Landscaping recently completed a full-scale front and backyard transformation at this stunning luxury property in Grange. The project was thoughtfully designed to combine aesthetic appeal with functionality, perfectly complementing the home’s architecture and lifestyle.

At the heart of the backyard design is a long raised garden bed, constructed using Versawall® blocks with capping. This feature not only adds structure and elegance but also provides a secure and elevated space for both decorative and edible plants, protected from the property’s two energetic dogs.

A serene crazy paving corner, laid with premium Travertine natural stone, offers a peaceful spot to unwind while taking in views of the pool. This same Travertine was used for the pool paving, chosen for its luxurious appearance and cool-to-the-touch surface—ideal for hot summer days.

A custom-built timber pergola creates a shaded retreat beside the pool, while a frameless glass pool fence ensures safety without compromising the open, resort-style aesthetic.

Basalt stepping stones, wet-laid alongside the garden beds, guide movement through the space while adding texture and contrast.

The Hybrid TifTuf lawn, known for its drought tolerance, high wear resistance, and low maintenance needs, was installed for a lush, year-round green finish.

In the front and rear of the property, Good Neighbour fencing offers privacy and durability. The front boundary is enhanced with modern vertical and horizontal slat fencing, complete with automated gates, providing both security and sophisticated curb appeal.

Finally, the garden beds were enriched with premium soil and carefully selected plants, all supported by a Wi-Fi-controlled, fully automatic irrigation system, ensuring optimal growth with minimal effort.

This Grange project is a showcase of modern, functional, and elegant outdoor living—crafted with precision by NextLevel Landscaping.
