# NextLevel Landscaping photo library

Curated 24 October 2026. Source material only: nothing in this folder has been integrated into `site/` or `demo/`.

## Summary

- Seven collections: six named projects plus mixed landing-page photos; **60 selected photos**, each with a main JPEG and a 600px-wide companion (**120 JPEGs**).
- Reviewed all **156 source images** visually, then reviewed selected views at larger size. **96 photos skipped**: 40 for privacy and 56 for repetition, lighting, composition or uncertain project context. Individual reasons appear below.
- Seven MOV files: video available, not processed. No video or original source file is included.
- Three client descriptions copied byte-for-byte; they are client-supplied claims, not independently verified specifications.
- EXIF rotation applied, source metadata stripped, progressive RGB JPEGs at quality 75. Main images have a maximum 1600px long edge; detailed scenes are resized further where needed to stay below 250 KB. No upscaling. Companion images are exactly 600px wide with the same crop/aspect ratio.
- Main-image target is 120–250 KB; simpler scenes and lower-resolution originals can be smaller. Thumbnails are intentionally smaller.
- Captions describe visible work. `after` means the visible landscaping or enclosure is complete, not proof of formal project handover. Irrigation is mentioned in the client descriptions but is not claimed as visible in any image.
- Privacy crops remove vehicle/address backgrounds where practical. Uncertain photos are excluded conservatively; privacy exclusions do not assert that every suspected detail was legible.
- Mixed landing-page photos have no confirmed project locations; do not assign suburb/project names from appearance. Penfield sources include both an industrial before-series and residential planting views; do not present them as an exact before/after pair without client confirmation.
