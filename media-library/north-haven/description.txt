NextLevel Landscaping recently completed a striking front yard transformation at this new corner property in North Haven. With a large, open space extending to the verge, the goal was to create a visually appealing and low-maintenance landscape that maximized street presence and functionality.

A carefully selected and thoughtfully designed paving pattern forms the foundation of the front yard, adding elegance, texture, and lasting value to the property. The layout enhances the home's frontage while guiding movement through the space naturally.

To add depth and structure, we built a raised garden bed and integrated steps using Versawall® blocks with matching split caps, achieving a clean and contemporary finish.

For ongoing ease of care, high-quality artificial grass was installed across the front yard, offering a lush green appearance all year round with minimal upkeep.

To further refine the look and define plant zones, LinkEdge aluminium steel edging was installed around the existing trees—neatly separating the grass from tree surrounds and enhancing the overall layout.

This North Haven project is a prime example of how smart design, quality materials, and expert craftsmanship can turn an open, underutilized space into a functional and eye-catching landscape.
