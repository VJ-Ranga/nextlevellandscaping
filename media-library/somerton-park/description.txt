At NextLevel Landscaping, we value long-term relationships with our clients, and this recent project in Somerton Park is a perfect example. A few years ago, we had the pleasure of enhancing this property’s front yard, where we constructed a stylish front wall, installed automated gates, built raised garden beds, and paved the entire front area—all designed to deliver much needed privacy, welcoming appeal with lasting impact.

Recently, our team was invited back for the next stage of the backyard transformation, focused on creating a relaxing, low-maintenance retreat.

We laid a concrete base to support the client's new spa, and built a custom composite deck around it—combining durability with sleek, modern aesthetics. Surrounding the space, we constructed raised garden beds using rendered Besser block walls, adding structure and a clean, contemporary look.

To complement the garden beds, a built-in L-shaped timber bench seat was installed, offering a warm, inviting spot to relax. A small patch of artificial turf was also added to bring a touch of green to the area—achieving a lush look without ongoing maintenance.

As with the earlier stage of the project, NextLevel Landscaping handled all council approvals on behalf of the client, ensuring a smooth and hassle-free process from start to finish.

This project reflects our commitment to quality, functionality, and design continuity—delivering results that evolve with our clients’ needs over time.
